    📷 QR Code Generator

    Type text or a URL, get a scannable QR code, download it as a PNG.

    How to use

    1. Enter the text or URL you want the QR code to contain.
    2. Pick a size and click Generate QR code.
    3. Scan it with your phone camera to test, then Download PNG.

    FAQs

    What can I put in a QR code?

    Any short text: website URLs, plain messages, Wi-Fi credentials, or contact details. Keep it under a few hundred characters for the most reliable scans.

    Do the QR codes expire?

    No. The code simply encodes the text you entered — there's no link-tracking server involved, so it works forever and offline.

    Is my content uploaded anywhere?

    No. The QR code is drawn locally in your browser. Nothing you type leaves your device.
