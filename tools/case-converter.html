FAQs

What is the difference between Title Case and Sentence case?

Title Case capitalises the first letter of every word ("Hello World"). Sentence case capitalises only the first letter of each sentence ("Hello world. How are you?").

Does it handle numbers and symbols?

Yes — numbers, punctuation and symbols pass through unchanged. Only letters are affected by the conversion.

Is there a character limit?

No. The conversion runs entirely in your browser, so even very long documents convert instantly.
