    📄 Text to PDF

    Convert plain text — pasted or uploaded — into a clean PDF document.

    🔒 Files never leave your browser. Everything is processed locally on your device.
    Share this tool:

    How to use

    1. Either drop a .txt file above or paste your text into the box.
    2. Click Convert to PDF — line breaks are kept as written.
    3. Your document.pdf downloads automatically, ready to share or print.

    FAQs

    Which input wins if I both upload and paste?

    The uploaded file takes priority. If you want to use pasted text instead, just pick a different file or reload the page and paste only.

    Is the formatting preserved?

    Line breaks and paragraph spacing are preserved, but this is plain text — there's no bold, italics, or font styling. For styled documents, try the HTML to PDF tool instead.

    Is my file uploaded anywhere?

    No. This tool runs entirely in your browser — your text is never sent to any server.
