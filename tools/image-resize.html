    FAQs

    Will enlarging a small image keep it sharp?

    Resizing uses smooth high-quality scaling, but enlarging a small image can't add detail that wasn't there — it may look softer. Downsizing stays crisp.

    Which format should I choose?

    PNG is lossless and best for graphics with sharp edges; JPEG keeps photos small; WebP usually gives the smallest file at good quality. The quality slider applies to JPEG and WebP.

    Is my file uploaded anywhere?

    No. This tool runs entirely in your browser — your file is never sent to any server.
