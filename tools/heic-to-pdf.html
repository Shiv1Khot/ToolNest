    📱 HEIC to PDF

    Bundle HEIC photos into a single PDF document.

    Add up to 50 photos. Each photo becomes one full-size page, in the order you selected them.
    🔒 Files never leave your browser. Everything is processed locally on your device.

    How to use

    1. Drop your HEIC photos into the upload box — you can select several at once.
    2. Press Convert. Each photo is decoded on your device and placed on its own PDF page.
    3. The finished PDF (named photos.pdf) downloads automatically.

    FAQs

    Can I convert multiple HEIC photos at once?

    Yes — select up to 50 photos in one go. They become full-size pages in a single PDF, in the order you picked them.

    How big will the PDF be?

    Each page is saved at 90% JPEG quality inside the PDF, so expect roughly the same total size as converting each photo to JPG individually.

    Will the PDF pages match my photo dimensions?

    Yes — every page is sized to its photo's exact width and height, so nothing is cropped or stretched.

    Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

    No. Both the HEIC decoder and the PDF builder run inside your browser — your photos are never sent to any server.
