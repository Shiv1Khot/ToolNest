    FAQs

    How are duplicates handled?

    Emails are deduplicated case-insensitively (Ada@Example.com and ada@example.com count as one), and phone numbers are deduplicated after stripping formatting like spaces, dashes and brackets.

    What counts as a phone number?

    Candidates with 7 to 15 digits are kept, so international numbers with country codes work while short numeric codes are skipped.

    Is my file uploaded anywhere?

    No. This tool runs entirely in your browser — your file is never sent to any server.
