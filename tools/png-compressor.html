    How to use

    1. Drop a PNG into the box above (or click to choose one).
    2. Drag the scale slider — 85% is a gentle shrink that looks identical on screen.
    3. Hit Compress & Download and save the lighter PNG.
    4. Need it even smaller? Drop the slider further — 50% quarters the pixel count.

    FAQs

    Why a scale slider instead of quality?

    PNG is a lossless format, so there's no quality dial — shrinking dimensions is what actually reduces the file. Fewer pixels means less data to store, and because nothing is discarded, edges stay razor-sharp.

    Will the image look worse?

    The pixels that remain are pixel-perfect copies of the original. It only looks smaller — there's no blur or blockiness like you'd get from JPEG compression.

    Is my PNG uploaded anywhere?

    No. This tool runs entirely in your browser — your file is never sent to any server.
