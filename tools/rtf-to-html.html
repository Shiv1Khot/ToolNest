    📝 RTF to HTML

    Convert RTF documents to clean, readable HTML.

    🔒 Files never leave your browser. Everything is processed locally on your device.

    How to use

    1. Drop your .rtf file onto the upload area.
    2. Click Convert to HTML — the readable text appears in the preview.
    3. Copy the HTML to your clipboard or download it as a standalone .html file.

    FAQs

    Does the formatting carry over?

    Only partly. This tool extracts the readable text and its basic paragraph structure — fancy formatting, tables, and embedded images don't survive the conversion. Think of it as a clean text rescue, not a pixel-perfect export.

    What can I do with the downloaded HTML?

    It's a complete, self-contained HTML file — open it in any browser, paste it into a CMS, or use it as a starting point for further editing.

    Is my file uploaded anywhere?

    No. This tool runs entirely in your browser — your file is never sent to any server.
