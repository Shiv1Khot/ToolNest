    📊 Excel to CSV

    Convert Excel workbooks to CSV files, one sheet at a time.

    🔒 Files never leave your browser. Everything is processed locally on your device.
    Share this tool:

    How to use

    1. Drop an .xlsx, .xls or .csv file onto the box above.
    2. Choose which sheet to convert from the sheet picker.
    3. Click Convert to CSV — you'll see the first 8 rows before downloading.
    4. Hit Download .csv to save your file.

    FAQs

    Can I convert all sheets at once?

    Not yet — each sheet becomes its own CSV file, so pick the sheet you need and convert it. You can repeat the process for other sheets.

    Will my formatting and formulas survive?

    Formula results come through, but formatting, charts and macros do not — a CSV stores plain text values only.

    Is my file uploaded anywhere?

    No. This tool runs entirely in your browser — your file is never sent to any server.
