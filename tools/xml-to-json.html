    FAQs

    How are attributes and repeated elements handled?

    Attributes land under an "@attributes" key, and repeated sibling elements become arrays — so <item>a</item><item>b</item> becomes "item": ["a", "b"].

    My XML won't parse — what now?

    The converter uses your browser's XML parser and shows its error message verbatim. The most common culprits are unescaped ampersands (& should be &amp;) and unclosed tags.

    Is my data uploaded anywhere?

    No. Conversion happens entirely in your browser — your data is never sent to any server.
