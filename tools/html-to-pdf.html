    📄 HTML to PDF

    Paste HTML and get a real PDF document, styled as written.

    🔒 Files never leave your browser. Everything is processed locally on your device.

    How to use

    1. Paste your HTML into the box — the preview underneath shows how it will look.
    2. Tweak the markup until the preview looks right (keep it self-contained: inline styles work best).
    3. Click Convert to PDF to download an A4 document.pdf.

    FAQs

    Why didn't my images or web fonts show up?

    The converter can only use what the page can load. External images and fonts are often blocked by cross-origin rules, so paste self-contained HTML — inline styles and data-URI images give the most reliable results.

    What page size is the PDF?

    A4, portrait orientation, with a 10 mm margin. Content flows across pages automatically.

    Is my file uploaded anywhere?

    No. This tool runs entirely in your browser — your HTML is never sent to any server.
