🗑️ Remove PDF Pages

Delete the pages you don't need and keep a clean, smaller PDF.

e.g. 2, 4-6
🔒 Files never leave your browser. Everything is processed locally on your device.

How to use

  1. Drop a PDF onto the upload box — you'll see how many pages it has.
  2. Type the pages you want gone, like 2, 4-6.
  3. Press Remove pages to download the trimmed PDF.

FAQs

What can I type in the pages box?

Single pages (2), ranges (4-6), or any mix separated by commas: 2, 4-6, 9. Spaces are fine, and overlapping entries are tidied up automatically.

Can I undo this?

Your original file is never changed — the tool reads it and writes a brand-new PDF without the pages you picked. Still, it's wise to keep a backup copy if you might want those pages back later.

Is my file uploaded anywhere?

No. This tool runs entirely in your browser — your file is never sent to any server.
