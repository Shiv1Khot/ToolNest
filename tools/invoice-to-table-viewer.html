🧾 Invoice OCR Reader

OCR an invoice photo or PDF into editable text with a line-item preview.

Preparing…

A quick word of caution: this is basic OCR, not AI line-item extraction. Text recognition makes mistakes — always check the figures before using them anywhere important.
🔒 Files never leave your browser. Everything is processed locally on your device.

How to use

  1. Drop an invoice photo (JPG/PNG) or a PDF onto the box above.
  2. Click Extract Text and wait while the OCR runs — you'll see live progress.
  3. Fix any misread characters in the editable text box, then download the detected lines as CSV.

FAQs

How accurate is the text recognition?

This is basic OCR, not AI line-item extraction — it reads the visible characters as best it can. Clear, straight-on scans work well; crumpled photos or tiny fonts will have errors. Always check figures before using them.

Which pages of a PDF are read?

Only the first page is scanned. If your invoice spans multiple pages, save the page you need as a separate PDF or image and upload that.

Is my invoice uploaded anywhere?

No. OCR runs entirely in your browser — your invoice is never sent to any server.
