FAQs

How is the exact age calculated?

Full years are counted first, then the remaining whole months, then the leftover days — the same way official documents compute age, accounting for leap years automatically.

What if I was born on 29 February?

In non-leap years your birthday is counted on 1 March for the countdown, and your age still increments correctly on the anniversary.

Is my birth date stored anywhere?

No. The calculation happens in your browser and nothing is sent or saved.
