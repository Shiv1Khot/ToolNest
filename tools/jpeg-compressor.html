How to use

  1. Drop a JPEG photo into the box above (or click to choose one).
  2. Drag the quality slider — try 80 for everyday photos.
  3. Hit Compress & Download and save the smaller file.
  4. Still too big? Compress the downloaded copy again at a lower quality.

FAQs

Will I notice the difference?

At quality 80–85, almost certainly not. That range strips out detail your eyes can't see anyway, which is why JPEG is the perfect format for photos of people, places, and products.

Does it resize my photo?

No — the width and height stay exactly the same. Only the compression level changes, so the photo keeps its original dimensions.

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. This tool runs entirely in your browser — your file is never sent to any server.
