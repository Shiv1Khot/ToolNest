    Convert JSON into human-friendly YAML.

    How to use

    1. Paste your JSON into the box above.
    2. Click Convert to YAML.
    3. Copy the YAML or download it as a .yaml file for your configs.

    FAQs

    Is the YAML valid?

    Yes — it uses block style with 2-space indentation, and strings that could be misread (things that look like numbers, booleans, or contain special characters) are quoted so nothing changes meaning.

    Can it handle deeply nested data?

    Yes, nesting is unlimited — each level just indents two more spaces. Very large documents may take a moment in the browser.

    Is my data uploaded anywhere?

    No. Conversion happens entirely in your browser — your data is never sent to any server.
