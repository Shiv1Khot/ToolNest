    📱 HEIC to PNG

    Convert iPhone HEIC photos to PNG without uploading them.

    🔒 Files never leave your browser. Everything is processed locally on your device.

    How to use

    1. Drop a HEIC photo into the upload box, or click it to pick a file.
    2. Press Convert — the decoder loads once, then converts on your device.
    3. The PNG downloads automatically, lossless and ready for editing.

    FAQs

    When should I choose PNG over JPG for a HEIC photo?

    Pick PNG when you plan to edit the photo further or want zero additional compression loss. For sharing and storing, JPG is usually smaller.

    Will the PNG be much bigger than the HEIC?

    Yes — HEIC is a very compact format and PNG stores every pixel losslessly. The upside is perfect quality with no extra compression damage.

    Do HEIC live photos or bursts convert too?

    The still image converts normally. Live-photo video clips and multi-frame bursts aren't carried over — you get the main photo frame.

    Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

    No. The decoder runs inside your browser — your photo is never sent to any server.
